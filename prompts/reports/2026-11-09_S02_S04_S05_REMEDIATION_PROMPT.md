### Verified Findings in Slices S02, S04 and S05

1. **S02-15 (Priority: Medium) – `crates/humoco-node/src/storage/engine.rs:928` and `:1301`**
   - **Expected vs. Actual Behavior:** Spec 14 section 4.1 requires identical in-flight approvals to coalesce with bounded waiters. After completed operation A is pruned and retry B reuses its key, A's waiter destructor unconditionally removes B; A's background completion task independently does the same. An identical C then enqueues another persistence operation instead of joining B.
   - **Reliability Impact:** Pending counters underreport outstanding work, and duplicate retries receive separate waiter budgets and queue entries. Independent service semaphores and the persistence channel still bound work; premature signatures or conflicting own choices were not demonstrated.
   - **Verified Trace:** Commit A with `DualTierEngine::flush_batch`, leave its future unpolled, and enqueue B for the same key. Resuming A changes `pending=1, queue=1` to `pending=0, queue=1`; enqueueing C produces `pending=1, queue=2`. Yielding to A's background cleanup reproduces the same defect separately.

2. **S04-19 (Priority: Medium) – `crates/humoco-node/src/network/transport.rs:3381`**
   - **Expected vs. Actual Behavior:** `send_f2f_operator_message` promises queueing and sending; its reconnect outbox must already contain the message when its authenticated ACK is processed. The implementation awaits transmission before `queue_outbound` at line 3391. The independent ACK task at line 4267 can run first, and `F2fInboxStore::on_ack_received` (`network/f2f_collab.rs:215`) discards the unmatched ACK.
   - **Reliability Impact:** The sender subsequently stores an already acknowledged message as pending with `delivered=false`, causing incorrect delivery status and reconnect retransmission. Cancellation between transmission and local publication can leave a sent message without its local record. This concerns operator diagnostics, not consensus finality.
   - **Verified Trace:** Executing the permitted `on_ack_received(friend,42)` then `queue_outbound(friend,message42,false)` ordering against the production store yields `early_ack_retained=false, pending_after_ack=true, delivered=false`. No end-to-end QUIC reproduction of this specific race was executed during the audit.

3. **S05-15 (Priority: High) – `crates/humoco-node/src/ingress/tier.rs:370` and `:560`**
